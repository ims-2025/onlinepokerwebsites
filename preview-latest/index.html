What is the best online poker site for US players in 2026?

Americas Cardroom (ACR) tops our rankings for US players thanks to the largest player pool available offshore, the multi-million-dollar Venom tournament series and a mature rewards program. Are these online poker sites legal in the US and Canada?

The sites we review operate offshore and are not licensed by US state regulators. A few US states run their own regulated poker rooms, while others rely on offshore options. Canada is broadly permissive for players. Laws vary by location and change over time, so it is your responsibility to confirm what is allowed where you live. How do we rank online poker sites?

We weigh player traffic and game quality, welcome-bonus and rakeback value, banking speed and fees, software and mobile experience, and each operator's track record and reputation. We update rankings as offers and conditions change.
